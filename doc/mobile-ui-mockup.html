Chapter 02
How Habits Shape Your Identity

Many people think that changing habits is about altering behaviour. But there's a deeper layer: identity. The most effective way to change who you are is to change what you believe about yourself.

Key Idea
Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become. No single vote decides the election — but the accumulation shifts everything.

Outcomes-based habits focus on what you want to achieve. Identity-based habits focus on who you wish to become. The goal isn't to read a book; it's to become a reader.

"The real reason habits matter is not because they can get you better results, but because they can change your beliefs about yourself."

— James Clear

Each time you write a page, you are a writer. Each time you practice, you are a musician. Habits are the path through which identity is built — one small action at a time.
